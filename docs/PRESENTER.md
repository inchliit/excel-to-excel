# Presenter cheat sheet: Excel to Excel

**Deck link (share in the webinar chat):** https://inchliit.github.io/excel-to-excel/

## Before you go live (5 min)
1. Open the link in Chrome on the laptop you present from. Click once inside the slide so it has focus.
2. Press **S**: the speaker-notes window opens (notes, timer, next slide). Allow pop-ups if Chrome asks.
3. In Zoom/Meet/Teams, **share only the deck tab/window**, never the notes window.
4. Press **F** for full screen on the deck. Paste the link in chat; the title slide also has a QR code.

## Keys while presenting
| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| → or Space | next animation step (one click = one step), then next slide |
| ← | back one step (the slide rebuilds exactly) |
| ↓ / ↑ | into / out of a trick's **Try it** sub-slide (only tricks with the yellow "↓ Try it yourself" badge) |
| Esc or O | overview of all slides; click one to jump |
| S | speaker notes window |
| B or . | black screen (pause) |
| F | full screen |

The agenda slide and the recap slide are clickable: click any trick to jump to it.

## Timing (90 min)
Intro 5 · Act 1 ~17 · Act 2 ~21 · Act 3 ~14 · Act 4 ~18 · Power Query teaser 2 · Q&A 13.
If you run late, skip the Try-it sub-slides (students can do them after; the link stays up).

## Try-it moments
On a trick with the badge, press ↓, tell students to open the same trick on their device, and give them 60–90 s.
They type the formula and press Enter; the box says Correct / Not quite with a hint. **Show answer** fills it in.
If a student's network blocks the checker, the box shows the formula and the expected result to try in Excel.

## If something breaks
- A trick slide shows a plain card instead of the animation → keep talking from the card; it has the formula/keys.
- Deck link down or no internet → open `excel-to-excel.pdf` (backup, every trick's final state).
- Refreshing the page keeps you on the same slide (the trick restarts at its first step).
